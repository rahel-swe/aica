# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

AICA is a learning and career guidance project. We want the community to be practical, respectful, and welcoming to contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Expected behavior:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but accept correction.
- Give feedback on work, not on people.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.
- Help make the codebase easier to understand.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or repeated hostile comments.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Spamming issues, pull requests, or discussions.
- Derailing project spaces with unrelated arguments.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its contributors.

If you need to report a conduct issue, contact the maintainers through a private channel when available. If no private channel is listed yet, open a minimal issue asking for a maintainer contact without including sensitive details.
